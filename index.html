Gradient Descent: Pricing Houses in Pune

Aim Theory Procedure Simulation Contributors

Aim

You are the data analyst at HomeLens, a Pune real-estate startup. Buyers keep asking one question: "What should a flat of this size cost?" Your job is to teach a model to answer it, and gradient descent is how the model learns.

By the end of this experiment you will be able to

  • Fit a line price = w × area + b to housing data by repeatedly reducing the prediction error.
  • Read the gradient as "which way, and how steeply, does the error grow?" and explain each parameter update.
  • Predict what happens when the learning rate is too small, well chosen, or too large.
  • Explain why feature scaling makes training faster and more stable.
  • Compare batch, stochastic and mini-batch gradient descent on the same data.

The 40 houses in this lab are synthetic: they were generated to look like a typical city market (about ₹4,000 per sq ft plus noise), not scraped from real listings.
